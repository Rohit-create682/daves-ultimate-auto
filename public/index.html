Hybrid/EV Service in the ATX

Quality repairs and exceptional service shouldn't be exclusive to gas and diesel engines and their drivers. Dave's Ultimate Automotive is the Dealership Alternative for hybrids and EV's including Tesla, Domestic, Asian, and European Import. We are seeing more and more EV's on Austin's roads today. Dave's understands the importance and need to stay updated with the latest in training, technology, and equipment. As a Dave's EV client, you will enjoy the same Dave's VIP benefits including shuttle service, loaner vehicles, after hours pickup and drop-off, and even financing!

Hybrid/EV Maintenance

A great benefit to owning an EV is the lower cost of maintenance. Just because there isn't an oil filter to change, doesn't mean to forget about maintaining your ride. Tire maintenance is one of the most important areas of care. Routine tire rotations, balancing, and air pressure checks combined with maintaining proper wheel alignment are critical to ensure proper rolling resistance. Brake systems are used differently and brake pads can last much longer, but did you know there are actually brake services required for EV's in between brake pad replacements?

Hybrid/EV Repair

If your EV is having problems, let Dave's EV Specialists take the worry out of your experience. At Dave's, we are qualified to diagnose and repair a multitude of problems. Addressing EV problems early is a critical step to managing costs but should your EV need mechanical repair, our expert technicians will only install factory quality or better parts.

For a one-time $10 sticker per vehicle, you'll receive 50% off oil changes for the life of that vehicle. This is over $45 in savings per oil change, up to 4 times a year! No recurring fees. No expiration. Just consistent savings every time you come in.

How It Works

  1. Purchase Your Sticker – For only $10 per vehicle

Why Join the Half-Priced Oil Change Club?

The $10 investment pays for itself on your very first oil change. After that, every visit puts money back in your pocket. Over the lifetime of a vehicle, members can save hundreds — even thousands — of dollars on routine maintenance.

It's one of the simplest and smartest ways to reduce the total cost of vehicle ownership.
